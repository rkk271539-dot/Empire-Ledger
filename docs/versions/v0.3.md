### 1.2 Section 18A Stock Price Dynamics
- **Heston Stochastic Volatility:** Instantaneous daily variance with mean reversion to sector variance targets and vol-of-vol diffusion.
- **Regime Switching:** Markov transitions across Calm (0), Normal (1), and Stressed (2) states. In stressed regimes, correlation between sectors rises via `stressedCorr()`.
- **Correlated Factor Shocks:** 9x9 Cholesky decomposition generating correlated market and sector returns.
- **Square-Root Price Impact Law:** Permanent ($0.65 \times \text{impact}$) and temporary ($0.35 \times \text{impact}$) price impact where:
  $$\text{impact} = Y \times \sigma \times \sqrt{\frac{q}{\text{adv}}}$$
- **Circuit Breaker Limits:** Daily return clamped to $\pm 10\%$ ($\ln 0.9$ to $\ln 1.1$).
- **Fundamental Mean-Reversion:** Prices drift toward intrinsic P/E and DCF fundamental valuation with regime-dependent speed.

## 2. Valuation Spike Bugfix (The 7,000+ Cr Bug Hunt)

### 2.1 The Problem
In v0.2, completing a lump-sum client project milestone (e.g. ₹2.5 Cr payout) caused EBITDA to report ₹2.5 Cr for that single day. The original annualized valuation calculation multiplied daily EBITDA directly by 365:
$$\text{Annualized EBITDA} = \text{EBITDA}_{\text{day}} \times 365 = ₹2.5\text{ Cr} \times 365 = ₹912.5\text{ Cr}$$
Applying an enterprise multiple of $12\times$ produced an explosive spike:
$$\text{Valuation} = ₹912.5\text{ Cr} \times 12 \approx ₹10,950\text{ Cr}!$$

### 2.2 The Solution
1. **Trailing 30-Day Run Rate:** `stepCompanyDaily` now tracks rolling 30-day historical financial statements (`history30d`). Annualized revenue and EBITDA are derived from the 30-day moving average, smoothing lump-sum contract completions over time.
2. **Valuation Exponential Moving Average (EMA):** Applied a 90% previous / 10% target valuation EMA filter to eliminate abrupt discontinuities.
3. **Daily Valuation Circuit Clamp:** Implemented a strict $\pm 6\%$ daily circuit clamp on company enterprise valuation moves.
4. **Automated Valuation Guard Test:** Added a test simulating 100 consecutive days of company operations and verified that early-stage valuation stays strictly below ₹1,000 Cr.
