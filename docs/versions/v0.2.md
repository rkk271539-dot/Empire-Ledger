# Version 0.2 — Economy Core Release Notes

## Scope & Deliverables
The goal of v0.2 is to implement the player's flagship operating company, internal departments, workforce management, active project pipeline, proprietary products, and comprehensive financial statements according to Section 3, Section 7.2, and Section 18A.5 of `GAME_SPEC.md`.

In addition, as requested, a complete **Founder Setup & Onboarding Registry** has been added at game start to configure founder details, enterprise sector, partner, rival, and upload custom photos.

### 1. Founder Setup & Onboarding Registry
- Added interactive onboarding modal (`FounderSetupModal.tsx`) shown on first launch or accessible from Settings / TopBar:
  - Custom founder name and headquarters city (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad).
  - Photo upload for Founder with live base64 image preview.
  - Flagship company name and sector selection.
  - Partner / Wife name and photo upload.
  - Arch Rival name and photo upload.
  - Persisted in state flags (`gameSetupCompleted`) and displayed across all relevant UI components.

### 2. Flagship Enterprise & Departments (`/src/engine/company/`)
- **Departments Hub**:
  - `marketing`: Generates new client project proposals; manages branding budgets.
  - `hr`: Manages talent acquisition, keeps workforce morale high, mitigates attrition.
  - `operations`: Converts employee skills into project execution work units; scales delivery throughput.
  - `rd`: Researches and develops product quality; drives unit cost efficiencies.
  - `finance`: Controls overhead and working capital.
  - `management`: Assigns available workforce to active client projects.
- **Workforce & Employee Management**:
  - Full employee model with skills (1-10), daily salaries, morale bars, and productivity factors.
  - Actions supported: `HIRE_EMPLOYEE`, `FIRE_EMPLOYEE` (with severance payouts), `TRAIN_EMPLOYEE` (skill & morale boost), `PROMOTE_EMPLOYEE` (morale maximized to 100%, 25% salary increase).
- **Client Projects Pipeline**:
  - Active deliverables that require workforce units to progress.
  - Marketing continuously surfaces client project proposals.
  - Deliveries generate major lump-sum cash payouts into corporate treasury, subject to client default risk checks.
- **Proprietary Product Lines**:
  - Generates steady daily recurring revenues with volatility capped at ±10%/month per Section 7.2.
  - Ability to commercialize new product lines (`LAUNCH_PRODUCT`).
- **Financial Statements & Valuation**:
  - Daily & Monthly P&L breakdown (Revenue, COGS, Payroll, Marketing, R&D, Overhead, Operating EBITDA, Net Income).
  - Simplified Balance Sheet (Treasury Cash, Accounts Receivable, Equipment/IP Assets, Debt, Shareholder Equity).
  - Dynamic company valuation computed at 12x EBITDA + net cash.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria Verification
- [x] Player company produces believable financials over 100 days using Section 18A.5 earnings dynamics.
- [x] Initial setup form allows filling names, details, and uploading photos for Player, Wife, and Rival.
- [x] Workforce actions (hire, fire, train, promote) update headcount, cash, and morale accurately.
- [x] Projects progress and deliver lump-sum cash payouts on completion.
- [x] P&L and Balance Sheet update dynamically every day.
- [x] Company valuation and founder net worth scale realistically with business operations.
